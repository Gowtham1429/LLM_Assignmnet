Nimbus Cloud Storage — Billing FAQ

How is my monthly bill calculated?

Your bill is the sum of three components: storage cost (based on average GB stored across the month, per tier), request cost (per 1,000 API requests, with PUT/COPY/POST requests priced at $0.005 per 1,000 and GET/HEAD requests priced at $0.0004 per 1,000), and data transfer cost ($0.09 per GB for data transferred out to the internet, with the first 10 GB per month free).

Are there any free tiers?

New accounts receive 25 GB of Standard tier storage free for the first 12 months, along with 20,000 GET requests and 2,000 PUT requests per month during that period. After 12 months, or if usage exceeds these limits, standard pricing applies automatically.

How often am I billed?

Nimbus bills monthly, on the first day of the following month, via the payment method on file. Invoices are available as PDF downloads from the billing dashboard and are retained for 24 months.

What happens if a payment fails?

If a payment fails, Nimbus retries the charge after 3 days and again after 7 days. If both retries fail, the account is placed into a 15-day grace period during which data remains accessible but no new uploads are accepted. After the grace period, the account is suspended and data is deleted 30 days after suspension unless the balance is paid.

Can I get a discount for committed usage?

Yes. Customers who commit to a minimum of 50 terabytes stored for 12 months receive a 20% discount on the Standard tier rate. This is called a Committed Use Discount and must be arranged with the Nimbus sales team.
